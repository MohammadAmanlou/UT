## 👋 About This Repository

This repository is a structured archive of the academic work I have completed throughout my undergraduate studies in **Computer Engineering at the University of Tehran**.

Over the past five years, semester by semester, I tried to preserve as much of my coursework as possible instead of letting assignments, implementations, reports, and projects disappear into old folders.

What started as a personal archive gradually became a fairly comprehensive collection of:

<table>
<tr>
<td>📝 <b>Homework Assignments</b></td>
<td>💻 <b>Computer Assignments</b></td>
<td>🚀 <b>Course Projects</b></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>🧩 <b>Programming Exercises</b></td>
<td>📓 <b>Jupyter Notebooks</b></td>
<td>📄 <b>Reports & Documentation</b></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>✅ <b>Solutions & Implementations</b></td>

<td>📚 <b>Course Materials</b></td>

The main goal is simple:

> **Keep the work organized, make it easy to revisit, and hopefully make it useful to other students as well.**

## 🎯 Why This Repository Exists

University courses often involve hundreds of hours of work:

* solving assignments,
* debugging code,
* implementing algorithms,
* writing reports,
* designing projects,
* preparing experiments,
* and learning from mistakes.

Unfortunately, much of that work is often forgotten once a semester ends.

I wanted to avoid that.

This repository is my attempt to maintain a long-term archive that can serve as:

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 🧠 Personal Reference

A place to revisit concepts, implementations, and projects from previous semesters.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### 🎓 Educational Resource

## 👨‍🏫 Teaching Assistant Materials

Most of this repository focuses on **my own coursework as a student**.

I have also worked as a Teaching Assistant for several university courses, but this repository is **not intended to be a complete archive of my TA work**.

A small number of TA-related materials are already present in some folders, but many of the courses for which I served as a TA are not included here.

I may organize and add selected TA materials in the future.

## ⚠️ Academic Integrity

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This repository is intended for **learning, reference, and educational purposes**.

If you are currently taking one of these courses, please **do not directly copy solutions or submit them as your own work**.

Course assignments, policies, and implementations can also change significantly between semesters.

A better way to use this repository is:

1. 🧠 Try solving the problem yourself first.
2. 🔍 Use this repository as a reference when you are stuck.
3. ⚖️ Compare your approach with another implementation.
4. 💡 Understand *why* a solution works.
5. 💻 Write your own implementation.
6. 🎓 Follow your university's academic integrity policies.

---

## 🕰️ Older Projects & Dependencies

Some projects in this archive were implemented several years ago.

As a result, they may depend on:

* older library versions,
* deprecated APIs,
* old compilers,
* specific operating systems,
* course-specific infrastructure,
* outdated package versions,
* or dependencies that are no longer actively maintained.

> [!NOTE]
> This repository should primarily be viewed as an **archive of coursework as it was completed at the time**, rather than a collection of actively maintained software packages.

Some projects may therefore require minor modifications before running in a modern environment.

## 🌍 Language

Materials in this repository may be written in either:

* 🇬🇧 **English**
